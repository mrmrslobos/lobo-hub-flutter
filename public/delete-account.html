Delete Your FamilyHub Account

Last updated: March 1, 2026

FamilyHub by LoboHub gives you full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated data using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete from within the App

You can delete your account directly inside FamilyHub:

  1. Open Settings — Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the Dashboard.
  2. Scroll to "Reset Data" — At the bottom of the Settings page, tap Reset Data.
  3. Confirm deletion — You will be asked to confirm. Once confirmed, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from both your device and our servers.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. Please include the email address associated with your FamilyHub account.

Request deletion via email

Send to: privacy@lobohub.com — we will process your request within 7 business days.

Note for family owners: If you are the owner of a family, deleting your account will also delete the family and all data associated with it. Other family members will lose access to that family's shared data. Consider transferring family ownership before deleting your account.

Data Retention After Deletion

  • Immediately deleted: All account data, module data, and AI history are removed from our active database upon confirmation.
  • Backup retention: Residual copies in automated database backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Subscription records: If you had an active subscription, payment records are retained by the app store (Google Play or Apple) under their respective privacy policies. FamilyHub does not store payment information.
