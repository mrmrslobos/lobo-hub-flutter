FamilyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

FamilyHub ("we", "us", "the App") is a family management application developed by LoboHub. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and display name. Passwords are managed by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and are never stored in the application database.

b) Family & module data

The App stores data you voluntarily enter across its modules, including but not limited to:

  • Tasks, chores, and their completion status
  • Calendar events and scheduling details
  • Meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists
  • Budget categories and transactions
  • Fitness logs, workout plans, and health metrics (weight, steps)
  • Devotionals, prayer requests, and reading plans
  • Polls, rewards, and daily habits

c) AI interaction data

When you use AI-powered features (recipe suggestions, fitness plans, budget analysis, smart suggestions, etc.), the prompts you provide and the responses generated are stored in your account for your reference.

d) Device & notification data

If you enable push notifications on a mobile device, we store a device token and platform identifier (iOS or Android) to deliver notifications. These tokens are deleted when you log out.

e) Regional settings

Your family may configure regional preferences (country, language, timezone, currency, units). These are used solely for display formatting and are not used to track your location.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • GPS or precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history or third-party tracking cookies
  • Analytics or behavioral tracking data

This data is transmitted securely via HTTPS. Please review Google's Gemini API terms for details on how they handle this data.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL.
  • Data at rest is stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure with row-level security (RLS), ensuring each family can only access its own data.
  • A local copy of your data is cached on your device for offline access and is cleared upon logout.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Data Sharing Within Families

FamilyHub is designed for household use. Data you create within a family (tasks, events, meal plans, etc.) is visible to all members of that family. Personal data such as fitness logs and AI history is visible only to you.

7. Children's Privacy

FamilyHub is intended for use by families, including households with children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Family accounts should be created and managed by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can remove that information.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: View all your data within the App.
  • Correction: Edit your data directly in the App.
  • Deletion: Use the "Reset Data" option in Settings to delete your data from both your device and our servers. You may also contact us to request full account deletion.
  • Portability: Your data is stored in standard formats and can be exported upon request.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or reset your data, it is removed from our servers. Residual copies in automated backups are purged within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
